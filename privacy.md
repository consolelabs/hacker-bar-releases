# Hacker Bar, Privacy Policy

Hacker Bar does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- Reading history and preferences are stored locally on your Mac.
- Network requests go only to the Hacker News Algolia API, lobste.rs, and (in the direct build) the update feed on GitHub, solely to fetch stories and check for updates.
- No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs with data collection.

Contact: open an issue on the repository.
